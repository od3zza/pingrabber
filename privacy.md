Privacy Policy for PinGrabber
Effective Date: June 4, 2026

1. Introduction
PinGrabber ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Chrome Extension.

2. Data Collection and Usage
PinGrabber operates entirely locally on your device (browser). We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, or browsing history.
All media extraction and processing happen directly within your browser. We do not have servers, and no information is ever sent to us or any third parties.

3. Justification of Permissions
To function properly, PinGrabber requires specific browser permissions. Here is exactly how they are used:

activeTab & Host Permissions (*://*.pinterest.*/*): Used strictly to identify if the current active tab is a Pinterest page and to read the HTML structure of that specific page to locate the media files (video, image, or canvas).

scripting: Used to inject a temporary script into the active Pinterest tab to extract the media URL or record the canvas animation.

downloads: Used exclusively to save the extracted media file directly to your local computer's download folder.

4. Third-Party Services
PinGrabber does not use any third-party APIs, tracking scripts, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your email or contact information, any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
